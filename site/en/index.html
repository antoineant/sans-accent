Français

Tapez sans accents, écrivez sans fautes.

Type without accents, write without mistakes. Writing French on a QWERTY keyboard? Sans-Accent adds the accents as you type, in every app.

Download for Mac

Sans-Accent is free and runs on macOS 14 or later.

How it works

Accents appear on their own

Type “ecole” and it becomes “école” as soon as you move on to the next word. Sans-Accent knows more than 35,000 words and enough grammar to write “je suis allé” or “à cause” without asking you.

ecole→école

You choose when French is ambiguous

Some words have two spellings, like “a” and “à” or “ou” and “où”. A small list appears above the word: choose with ↑ ↓, then confirm with ⏎. If you keep typing, the word stays as it is.

àa↑↓ ⏎

One key for everything else

Tap ⌥ after any word to see its other spellings. You can also turn Sans-Accent off in a specific app, like your code editor.

⌥

What you type stays on your Mac

To add accents, Sans-Accent reads what you type, which is why macOS asks you to allow Accessibility access. Everything happens on your Mac. The app sends nothing, stores nothing and contains no trackers.

Sans-Accent is signed by its developer and notarized by Apple, so macOS checks that the app hasn't been altered since it was published.

Install Sans-Accent

  1. Download the file and open it.
  2. Drag Sans-Accent into your Applications folder.
  3. Open the app: a window walks you through allowing access, then lets you try it right away.
